# Code of Conduct

We aim to provide a respectful and collaborative environment for everyone who participates in our repositories.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, welcoming and professional.
- Respect different backgrounds, skill levels and perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback and accept feedback in good faith.
- Focus disagreements on technical decisions and evidence.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or the unauthorized publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers or through the contact options on our [official website](https://www.energiesolution.it/). Please do not post sensitive details in public issues.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, edit or remove inappropriate content, and restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as discreetly and fairly as possible.

This document applies to public interactions in organization repositories and related collaboration spaces.
